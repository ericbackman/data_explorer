Who paid the income tax

personal income tax only

Every individual return filed and assessed, grouped by total income. This is one slice of the ledger above — personal income tax, not the sales, payroll, corporate and property taxes that make up most of the rest. Administrative tax-year counts also don't reconcile line-for-line with the national accounts, so read this for its shape rather than against the totals above.

The bands are nominal dollars and are never re-indexed. $100,000 in 2018 is not $100,000 in 2024, so a rising share of filers in the upper bands is partly inflation moving people across fixed lines rather than incomes genuinely pulling away. Compare the shape of a single year confidently; compare across years only with that in mind.

The same income, taxed by province

Effective rate on total income for filers in one band, wherever they filed. Federal tax is the same everywhere, so the spread here is provincial rates, provincial credits, and the different mix of incomes inside a band that is thousands of dollars wide.

Spending per person, by province

different basis

Federal money is not in these bars. Statistics Canada publishes the all-levels view for Canada only; every province is available solely as provincial + local government combined. That cut is designed to be comparable across provinces — consolidation absorbs the differences in how each one organises service delivery — but it is a smaller pot than the ledger above, and the two should never be read against each other.
