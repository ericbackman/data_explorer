Federal money is not in these bars. Statistics Canada publishes the all-levels view for Canada only; every province is available solely as provincial + local government combined. That cut is designed to be comparable across provinces — consolidation absorbs the differences in how each one organises service delivery — but it is a smaller pot than the ledger above, and the two should never be read against each other.
