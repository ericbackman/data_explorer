## Soccer — World Cup draws (1,007 matches, 1930-2022)
**Status: de-hyped (verify ✗ → conclusion softened).** Notebook: `soccer_wc_draws.py`

- **Overall draw rate: 22.3%** (fair ≈ 4.48). **Group-stage: 24.2%** (fair ≈ 4.1). Knockout-to-pens: 17.1%.
- **By mismatch (group):** even 24.6% (n=305) · moderate 23.2% (n=285) · large/giant-vs-minnow 25.5% (n=153).

**Read:** the draw rate is **flat** across mismatch levels — all within a ±6% margin, statistically indistinguishable. The favorite's edge shows up as *fewer losses* (loss rate 31%→16% as the gap grows), **not fewer draws**. Your instinct that "the favorite is strong" is right; "so the draw is dead" is not.

> ⚠️ The "favorites-vs-minnows is a draw-betting angle at 4.5+" claim did **not** survive verification — the bucket difference is ~15× smaller than its sampling error and flips sign with the Elo parameter. Treat as a null result, not an edge. Hard gaps: **no odds table** (fair benchmarks only, can't prove mispricing) and **halftime scores 100% empty** (HT→FT impossible here).
