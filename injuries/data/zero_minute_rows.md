# Zero-minute rows in `player_game`

`player_game` holds 132,069 rows with `min = 0` across all season types and none NULL. They are not did-not-play rows. They are two different things split by the era in which the league's source started recording `plus_minus` (1996-97). Before 1996-97 (122,789 regular-season rows, 18.8% of the rows in those seasons) `min = 0` means minutes were not recorded: 117,976 of those rows carry real box-score stats (points, rebounds, assists, fouls) and all of them have `plus_minus` NULL; the share is highest in the 1950s and 1960s and falls to a few rows a season by the late 1980s. From 1996-97 on (3,225 regular-season rows, 0.44% of the rows) they are genuine sub-minute appearances: 1,450 have a non-zero `plus_minus`, so the player was on the court while points were scored, and 619 carry box-score stats; the other 1,515 are all zeros, which fits a few seconds on the floor rounded down to 0 minutes. The database therefore carries no did-not-play rows: the modern seasons average 104 zero-minute rows a season out of about 23,843 player-game rows, while DNP rows would add several per team-game. A player's games played in this database means games in which he appeared, so any missed-games count has to be derived by comparing a player's rows with his team's schedule.
